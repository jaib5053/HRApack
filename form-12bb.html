Under the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (effective April 1, 2026), Form 12BB is succeeded by Form No. 124 [See section 392(5)(b) and rule 205]. Key statutory updates in Form 124 include:

What is Form 12BB?

Form No. 12BB is a mandatory statutory statement introduced under Rule 26C of the Income Tax Rules, 1962. Salaried employees in India must submit Form 12BB to their employer at the end of each financial year (typically between December and February) to substantiate claims for tax exemptions under Section 192.

Important: Without submitting Form 12BB alongside valid rent receipts, your employer is legally mandated to deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) on your full salary without granting HRA exemption.

Particulars Required in Form 12BB for HRA

In the HRA section of Form 12BB, you must furnish the following 4 statutory details:

Particulars Required Statutory Requirement
1. Rent Paid to Landlord Total rent paid during the financial year (April 1 to March 31).
2. Name & Address of Landlord Full legal name of property owner and complete physical address of the rented premises.
3. Landlord's PAN Mandatory if rent exceeds ₹1,00,000/year (CBDT Circular 8/2013). If landlord has no PAN, attach Landlord Declaration.
4. Evidence / Rent Receipts Signed monthly rent receipts for each month claimed.
